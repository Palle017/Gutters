BEFORE YOU CHOOSE YOUR DOOR

Custom screen door questions

What should I send for a screen door estimate?

Send a photo showing the whole entrance, a closer view of the current door or opening, and approximate width and height if you have them. Include your location and favorite design. Approximate dimensions help start the conversation; John confirms final measurements and fit. Request a free estimate or text John at (239) 355-3686.

Can I ask for a custom size or double screen doors?

Yes. Talk to John about custom sizes and single or double screen doors. The suitable configuration depends on your opening, and John confirms the dimensions, design details, and arrangement for your project.

Should I replace my door or just replace the screen?

Tell John whether the problem is torn mesh, a door that does not work as it should, or the look of the entrance. Photos help him discuss repair, rescreening, or replacement. Door screens and sliding door screens are included in the rescreening and restoration service, and there is no minimum job size.

Are the design pictures completed installations?

No. The six door pictures and coastal house artwork are design visualizations, not completed-project photographs. The enlarged detail view is a crop of the same artwork. Use them to compare designs; John confirms the final appearance and project details with you.

How much does a custom screen door cost?

Ask for a free estimate for your opening and chosen design. John reviews the size, configuration, and work needed, then confirms the scope and amount. The artwork on this page is not a price quote.
