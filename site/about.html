Meet Jonathan Wisdom

Screen and gutter work for Lee County homes and contractor projects.

Our story

Everything Screen and Gutters is owned by Jonathan Wisdom and rooted in Lee County, Florida. Jonathan helps neighbors and builders with the outdoor trades that matter here: screen repairs, lanai rescreening, and gutter repairs and installation.

Whether you need a torn panel fixed, a full lanai rescreen, screen enclosure repair, or gutter repair and installation, we keep the focus on solid craftsmanship and clear communication.

Who we serve

Homeowners call when a screen is torn, a lanai needs rescreening, a screen enclosure needs attention, or gutters are failing. We keep the process simple: listen, estimate, and get the job done cleanly.

General contractors use us as a sub on screen and gutter scopes. If you’re managing a larger project and need a reliable trade partner in Lee County, we’re set up to work with you.
