JOHN’S PRACTICE

410 stainless steel screws to help prevent rust

Old standard metal screws rust. John’s practice is to remove old standard metal screws and use 410 stainless steel screws to help prevent rusting.

Rusted screws are a small job John is happy to talk about, whether on window screens, doors or a screen enclosure.

What to send John

Text or send a photo of each window screen that needs attention, plus a wide photo of the side of the home, and your property location. John can discuss repair or replacement during a free estimate.
