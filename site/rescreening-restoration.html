REPAIR OR RESCREEN?

Start with the screens you have.

A damaged screen panel and a whole enclosure with worn mesh are different projects. Send John a wide view of the enclosure, patio or window, then close photos of the damage. For a door, include the full opening and any hardware that needs attention.

John can discuss the condition and scope during a free estimate. You can ask about a small repair, replacement screen mesh or a larger rescreening job without committing to replacing every screen.
