Ask John about restoring your cage ↗
  1. Take out all the screen John starts off by taking all the screen out of the cage.
  2. Clear bushes near the cage He removes any bushes that might be too close to the cage.
  3. Replace all screws All screws are replaced.
  4. Pressure wash The cage is pressure washed.
  5. Protect the surrounding areas Then he preps the cage with drop cloths, tape, and everything necessary to protect the surrounding areas.
  6. Paint with Tough Trim aluminum paint John paints using Tough Trim aluminum paint. Want more on the paint and color options? See pool cage and aluminum painting.
  7. Re-screen the following day The following day, John re-screens the cage with whatever choice of screen is picked. Look at the screen types John uses, including Ultra Screen, and pick with him during your estimate.
