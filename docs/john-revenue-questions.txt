JOHN WISDOM — TEN QUESTIONS TO BUILD THE BUSINESS AROUND
GPT-6 Astra review, October 25, 2026

Ask these in order. Start with question 1.

1. Which type of job do you most want more of because it earns you good money for the time involved?

2. For that job, what problem is the homeowner really paying you to solve?

3. When a customer chooses you over another contractor, what do they say made the difference?

4. What is the most common concern that stops an interested customer from accepting your quote?

5. Which completed job best demonstrates the result a new customer can expect from you?

6. Where do you most often lose potential work: missed calls, before giving an estimate, or after sending the quote?

7. After a homeowner requests an estimate, what needs to happen before you can give them a quote they can approve?

8. How quickly can you consistently respond to a new inquiry while you are working on jobs?

9. Which neighborhoods or towns are most worthwhile for you to take jobs in?

10. When a customer has not accepted a quote, what follow-up has actually helped you win the job?
